Duchamp Journal is a non-profit science journal started by high-school students in Guangzhou and New York. We explain biology, biomedical science and engineering by asking why it matters, not just how it works — and every piece ships in Chinese and English, as text and as audio.

Our website is still being built. Until it opens, this page collects the English editions of our 微信公众号 posts, so you can see what we make before you decide to make it with us.
